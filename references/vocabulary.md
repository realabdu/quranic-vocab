Source: [Verified Quranic vocabulary Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1PLLtcgwbwtkOfpAZOotUQiZw-ARtZiAQuAkx2Z4D0XA/edit?gid=0#gid=0). Normalized on 2026-08-21.

This runtime table contains all 65 populated Sheet terms. Arabic text, definitions, categories, and research citations are intentionally excluded.

- `Canonical singular` is the Sheet's English-translation value.
- `Canonical +s plural` is the Sheet's `+s` plural. `—` means no canonical plural is defined.
- `Noncanonical forms` combines alternative spellings, literal translations, and Arabic-derived plurals, excluding blanks, dashes, and duplicates of canonical forms.
- A noncanonical form is a violation only when it denotes the Quranic concept.
